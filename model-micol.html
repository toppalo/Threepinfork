Micol

Micol is an Italian-born model and movement artist based in San Francisco, with a look that blends timeless European sophistication and modern fashion edge.

She brings a playful, cinematic energy to set, effortlessly shifting from soft, understated emotion to bold, editorial presence. With an intuitive sense for body language and detail, she's especially strong in fashion, beauty, and jewelry work, where subtle expression, elegant hands, and intentional posing make all the difference. Her versatility shines in luxury editorials, statement accessories, and detail-driven compositions that rely on mood, texture, and storytelling.
