Olivia

Olivia is a San Francisco–based runway and editorial model working within high fashion. Her look and movement are refined, versatile, and fashion forward.

Her runway credits include J. Ehren at the Beacon Grand Hotel and Brandin Vaughn (Beyoncé) with Alex Carter (Project Runway) at Levi's Plaza, San Francisco, as well as ongoing work with San Francisco Fashion Week.
